What you can do

Everything for a first game, in one tab.

01

Build with blocks

Add parts, move, scale and rotate them with your finger or mouse. Snap to a grid, group things into models, and undo anything.

02

Script it in Lua

Write Script, LocalScript and ModuleScript files. Put them in the same places a real game keeps them, and watch the Output window while you play.

03

Press Play

Jump into your world straight away with on-screen controls for touch screens. Stop, tweak, play again.

04

Save it, take it with you

Save with one button. Export a place file (.rbxlx) that opens in Roblox Studio, or publish it to your own Roblox experience with your own key.

How it works

Three steps from idea to playable.

  1. Pick a start. Flat ground, a ready-made Sky Islands course, or an empty place.
  2. Add and script. Place parts and give them behaviour with a few lines of Lua.
  3. Play and save. Test it right away. Press Save when you like it.

Made for small screens too

Questions

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and no account to make.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in your browser and start building.

Where are my places saved?

On VoxMesa's server, linked to the browser you used. Press Save to store your work; a draft is also kept on your device in case you lose connection.

Is VoxMesa made by Roblox?

No. VoxMesa is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Roblox and Roblox Studio are trademarks of Roblox Corporation, named here only to describe file compatibility.

Can I use my places in Roblox Studio?

You can export a place file (.rbxlx) and open it there, or publish to your own experience with your own Roblox Open Cloud key.

Ready to make something?

It takes about ten seconds to start.
